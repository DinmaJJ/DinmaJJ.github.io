## The problem

An e-commerce business had the data to understand its customers but no clear view of it. The questions: how are sales performing, how do customers buy, how reliable is delivery, and which customers are at risk of leaving?

## The data

Four datasets with more than **50,000 records** covering orders, customers, products and reviews.

## What I built

- A cleaning and SQLite ETL workflow that turns the four raw datasets into one analysis-ready model.
- Exploratory analysis of sales performance, purchasing patterns and product delivery.
- RFM segmentation (recency, frequency and monetary value) to group customers and pick out the most valuable ones.
- Machine-learning models for churn risk and customer lifetime value.
- Interactive Plotly visuals in a Streamlit app, so a non-technical team can explore the answers themselves.

## What it shows

Customers who had been inactive for **more than 180 days** were at a much higher risk of churning. That makes activity a practical early warning: the business can see who needs re-engaging before they're gone.

Alongside that, the RFM segments showed which customer groups bring the most value, so re-engagement effort can go where it matters.
